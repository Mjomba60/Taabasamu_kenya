# Taabasamu

This is a Non-Government Organization (NGO) within Kenya. It hearts from the Kibra a slum within the country. The region faces various challenges some communal while some are personal. Some of the challenges faced include but not limited to ;-

1. Insufficient meals amongst families.
2. High number of children lacking education.
3. Low standards of living.
4. Lack of proper equipped and accessible medical facilities.
5. Lack of sufficient support to youths with skills and abilities.

## Taabasamu's role in Kibra

We tend to become a beacon of hope to those families and communities within Kibra. Shinning a light to those who feel lost and a helping hand to those who require suppoort. We show the potential that slums hold if given the proper care and direction to take. Although we may not reach all the members in slum, we try to access almost all regions within the slum. This is possible through the programs and activites that we host as an organization in different parts of the slum. Some of the activities that we host include but not limited to ;-

1. Tournaments.
2. Seminars.
3. Food drives.
4. Empowerment fourms.
5. Community education.

Although we may not provide much, it is those who believe in our camapaign that keep our fire burning. We always welcome those who suppoort our vision and mission in whatever way they can. Some of the ways individuals and organizations who are obliged to help may be active include **Donations, Sponsorships, invting others to the organization**.
